# EAIA-1.0 dataset card

Collection date: 2026-08-14. This starter held-out corpus contains 16 hand-written, realistic
personal-narrative samples: 4 native human, 4 ESL-style human, 4 pure synthetic-style, and 4
hybrid/polished samples. The samples are original text authored for this project, so no external
license is claimed or required. The benchmark is a development signal, not a production accuracy
claim.

It does not cover supplemental short answers, STEM-focused essays, non-US application formats,
other languages, or a representative range of L1 backgrounds. The ESL quadrant is especially small
and its disparity estimate is low-confidence. Synthetic samples are style-controlled examples, not
outputs from a large multi-model collection.
